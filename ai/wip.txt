1) Personas
    have it make an email
    then have it make an email with a user/system prompt of what the persona is
    but notice how it made up some details
2) Context
    remember that its a prediction machine
    if you don't 
    reducing hallucinations
    include as musch detail as you can
    
        what you NEED from it
            Give me some ideas for a birthday present under 30$
            vs
            Give me 5 ideas for a birthday present/ My budget is $30. The gift is for a 29 year old who loves winter sports, and recently switched from snowboarding to skiiing

    don't skimp on details. be specific, and don't hold back.

    the downside of genAI is that they are eager to please, and so they will fill in the gaps.

    more context = less hallucinations

    but even when you do add more context, there is still a risk of it hallucinating. so.. how do we fix that?

    GenAI has powerful tools at their disposal. But, they are searching the web the same as we are, and could make the same mistakes, such as searching the wrong site, getting bad or old information.

    As it remembers, it will appear that it can remember everything. its not a human, its not going to remember everything you said. its a computer, guessing. 
        never assume it knows something. 
        never assume it has all the context.
        always provide all the context, every time

    give your AI permission to fail
        explicitly say: if you don't know, can't find the answer or its not in the context, ask me.
        if you don't say that, it will lie to give its best answer
        this is the #1 fix for hallucinations as of right now

3) format
    output requirements:
        2-Format: use a clear bulleted list for the timeline of events
        3-Length: keep it under 200 words
        4-Tone: Profession, apologetic, and radically transparent. No corporate fluff.

    Few shot prompting
        so far we've been telling it what we need, and giving it guardrails. 
        insstead of describing the output, we are SHOWING the output
        By giving it examples of how we want it to look, the genAI will have a MUCH better understanding of how we want it to be formatted

        example 1: technical transparency (provide example)
        example 2: Timeline Structure (provide example)
        example 3: Tone & Ownership (provide example)

4) Advanced Techniques

    a) Chain of Thought
        before (..) think through step by step, then provide the output
        accuracy goes right up
        its a prediction engine, and by having it verbalize, it will naturally come to the conclusion that makes the most sense
        secondly, you get to see exactly how it came to that conclusion
        its been so successful at improving accuracy that many new models have a "thinking" feature, called a "reasoning" model

        but this is old news, what's new technique?
    b) Trees of Thought
        how often does a single chain of thought lead to the right conclusion? 
        Often, you come to a dead end, a good option, or even a better option.
        But how do we do this with GenAI, a simple prediction engine?

        Please use the "Tree of Thoughts" reasoning framework to determin the mode effective way to accomplish this. Structure your response exact as follows:
        Step 1: Brainstorm three distinct (..) approaches for the (..)
        - Branch A: "Radical Transparency" - heavy emphasic on the detailed technical root cause and timeline
        - Branch B: "Customer Empathy First" - primary focus on acknowledging the business impact, frustation and erosion of trust
        - Branch C: "Future-focused assurance" - center the message almost entirely on what has been fixed and the new safeguards implemented.

        Step 2: evaluate each branch objectively:
        -list clear pros and cons for each approach
        - specifically analyse: which restores trust most effectively? which risks alienating or confusing non-technical customers? which might invite unwanted scrutiny or speculation?

        Step 3: Synthesize the best elements from all three branches to create an optimal "golden path" strategy that balances transparency, empathy, and confidence-building.

        Step 4: Using the Golden Path, write the complete customer apology email. The email should:
            be signed by (..)
            - have a clear, consice subject
            - use professional, calm and confident language
            - include appropriate sections (.....)
            - end on a high note of commitment and gratitude

        Show all your reasoning step-by-step before presenting the final email.
    c) playoff method (adveserial validation)
        you are the "prompt engineering Tournament Controller"
        your task is to generate the perfect (..) 
        to achieve this, simulate a 3-round competition between three distinct personas:
        Contestant 1: the engineer - obsessed with technical accuracy, transparency, and factual precision. Hates fluss and vagueness
        Contestant 2: the PR Crisis manager: focused on protecting brand repuation..
        COntestant 3: the angry customer: a CTO whos company just lost $50,000+ due to the outage. furious, zero tolerance for excuses, calls out coporate speak instantly. 
        
        Round 1: initial drafts
        - the engineer writes their version of the apology email
        - the PR Crisis manager writes their version of the apology email
        (do not write the angry customer yet)

        Round 2: the roast
        - the angry customer reads both drafts and brutally critiques them.
        - they must point out every excuse, every vague or evasive sentence, every tone-deaf moment, and anything that feels insincere or inadequate.

        Round 3: the synthesis
        - the engineer and PR Crisis manager read the angry customer's feedback.
        - they collaborate (you show their brief discussion if helpful) and produce ONE final, unified apology email that fully addresses every single criticism raised in round 2.
        output format:

        1) clearly label and present the full dialogue and drafts for all three rounds
        2) After round 3, separately display the final apology email in clean, ready-to-send format (with proper subject line, greeting, signature, etc.)
        (Begin the tournament now)
